MONOLITH - THE LOADING SCREEN
=============================

Starting the game now opens on your Oort splash picture, with a loading bar in the
HUD's style and a line saying what it is doing. When it is done the picture fades
into the mission.

What to look at
  * Is something on the screen from the moment the game can draw it (about 3.5 s
    after you start it here; before that it is Unity's own window and logo)?
  * Does the bar move at about the speed the loading really goes, and do the words
    say the right thing? (It is never on a timer: every step is real work.)
  * Is the start smooth when the picture fades? Nothing should stall in the first
    seconds of flight now (before: stalls of up to 0.8 s as the sky, the effects
    and the cabin were used for the first time).
  * Does the picture sit well on your screen? It is never stretched; on a wide
    screen it has a little black at the sides.

Play.bat [mission] [halo|meridian|cipher] [window] [noloading] [veryeasy|easy|medium|hard|insane]
  Play.bat                   escort, the style you chose last, fullscreen
  Play.bat cabin cipher      Dead Water, the bar in the Cipher style
  Play.bat dogfight window   in a window
  Play.bat escort noloading  the old start, no screen, to compare the two
The bar is drawn in whichever HUD style you play with (F9 changes it, hud.json keeps it).

Your own picture: put a file called splash.png in a folder called Loading next to Play.bat
(Loading\splash.png) and it is used instead. It is never stretched; a 16:9 picture fits best.

Files it writes next to the game: loading_stats.json (how long each step took, so the
next bar goes at your computer's pace). Delete it any time.

Sandbox.bat still opens the effects sandbox, now behind the same screen.
